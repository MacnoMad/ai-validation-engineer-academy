Become an AI Validation Engineer

This is a from-scratch, practitioner-level training program: 30 one-hour sessions, Monday–Friday, built to make you genuinely good at testing and validating AI-based systems — how they differ from conventional software, how to evaluate machine learning models, and how to test data, models, and pipelines like it's your job. Interactive labs, not just reading, and everything runs offline.

The curriculum borrows its scope and structure from an internationally recognized body of knowledge for AI testing, so the coverage is rigorous and nothing important gets skipped — and if you ever decide you want the official ISTQB Certified Tester AI Testing (CT-AI) credential to go with the skills, the last few sessions double as exam prep. That part is entirely optional; the skills stand on their own either way.

How the curriculum is weighted

Rather than spending equal time on every topic, sessions are weighted by how much a recognized industry body considers each topic to matter for a competent AI tester — Chapter 6 (Model Testing) carries the most weight of all, so this course spends extra sessions there. If you do go on to sit the official certification exam, this happens to line up exactly: 40 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, 65% to pass, with entry requiring the Foundation Level (CTFL) certificate — but that's a bonus, not the point.
